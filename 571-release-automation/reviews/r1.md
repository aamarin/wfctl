### Findings

WARNING  change-carries-release-note.md (Consequences), research.md §3, contracts/check-release-note.md — the release-branch exemption is open to anyone. The record says requiring the name and version to agree "keeps the name from working as a way around the check". It doesn't: a branch named `release/v0.23.0` cut from today's `main` (version 0.23.0) passes with no note, no deletion and any content. With `main` protected and `release-note` required, that is a one-line bypass of the gate this feature adds, for an agent or a person. → Also require that the version differs from (is greater than) the version at the merge base, and ideally that the branch deletes every note it started with. Add a test; fix the sentence either way.

WARNING  design/571-towncrier-builds-release-notes.md (Verified, Decision, Considered) vs research.md §4 — the level 3 record says towncrier "can't leave a category out" and has the script "remove the `none` notes first" then delete the remaining notes. research.md §4 measured the opposite (an unconfigured category is silently skipped, so leaving `none` out of `[[tool.towncrier.type]]` works) and the plan builds from a temporary copy instead. plan.md notes the divergence in its own prose, but the record is the durable thing and still carries both the wrong "can't" and the old mechanism. The rejected option, with its true reason (the check would need a second category list, and `towncrier create 571.none.md` refuses an unknown category), is exactly a Considered entry. → Reword Decision to the temp-copy mechanism, drop "can't" from Verified, and add the omit-`none` option to Considered.
