real exposure is the reviewer-discovery heuristic (L189–196): its input is

account, and its output is an `--add-reviewer` argument. Filed as a WARNING with
the ordering fix rather than a blocker, because the paragraph's closing sentence
already constrains it — it is just the last thing a skimmer reads instead of the
first. Nothing in the diff grants access or widens a permission.
