SKILL.md governs; these break first.
1. Answer on the first line. Justification is a follow-up.
2. Plain words before identifiers.
3. Depth is opted into by the words asked, never by the topic. Can't quote
   them? Stay short.
4. Say what the subject is before deciding about it.
5. Nothing that needs no decision gets a paragraph.
6. The answer plus one supporting block, then stop. A counted lead-in
   ("three things") is the tell.
No `##` or `###` in a reply; bold lead-in instead.
