### Findings

**WARNING** `wfctl/agents/skills/conversation-response-shape/digest.md:8` —
rule 6 drops the one word that makes the rule not self-contradict the rest of
`SKILL.md`. Precedence rule 6 in `SKILL.md` is *"The answer, plus at most one
supporting block **of prose**."* The digest renders it as *"The answer plus
one supporting block, then stop."* `SKILL.md`'s own "Show: the drawing is the
description" section spells out why "of prose" is load-bearing: *"A drawing
the material calls for does not count against that... The cap governs prose:
one block, not one of each kind."* Strip "of prose" and the digest reads as a
flat one-block cap that includes drawings — exactly backwards from what
`SKILL.md` argues for, and exactly the failure mode the digest exists to
prevent: a session running on the digest alone, deep into context decay,
sees only "one supporting block, then stop" and may suppress a diagram the
material calls for because it thinks the quota is spent. This is the one
place in the file that fails the change's own stated invariant ("a rule in
the digest that contradicts SKILL.md is a bug in the digest") → add "of
prose": `"6. The answer plus one supporting block of prose, then stop."` (5
more characters against a 479/500 budget — fits).
