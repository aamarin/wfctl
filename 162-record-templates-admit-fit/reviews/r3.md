## Findings

**WARNING** `architecture-decisions/SKILL.md:130-135` — the grandfathering rule is
stated unconditionally but argued only for `accepted` records, and six live records
fall in the gap. The paragraph opens "That rule outranks the section list" — "that
rule" being *An accepted record is immutable*, which by its own text binds only
records that reached `accepted` ("Once a record is `accepted`, exactly two things
ever change"). Sentence 1 then generalises with no status qualifier ("a record
written before a section existed does not carry it, and is not backfilled"), while
sentence 2 narrows the evidence back to accepted records. Under `docs/architecture/`
there are 14 level-2 records: 8 `accepted` and **6 `proposed`**
(`brainstorm-is-one-step-with-addressable-levels`,
`drift-is-measured-against-the-recorded-source`, `io-owns-durability-not-domain-files`,
`mirror-supersedes-the-wrapper`, `the-underscore-is-the-module-contract`,
`tracker-owns-the-issue-key-shape`). A `proposed` body is editable — this repo edits
them, two commits before this one (`33abcf7`, `aebeb1c`) rewrote
`the-underscore-is-the-module-contract`, which is `proposed`. So when a human comes
to accept one of those six, the new Verification item (`SKILL.md:150`) demands a
`Direct baseline` the record does not have, and the author has two contradictory
instructions: edit it in (allowed, the record is not immutable) or leave it
(sentence 1 says not backfilled). The issue's DoD — "decided once and written down"
— is met for the eight the issue counted and unresolved for the six it missed.
→ Fix: one clause naming the status. Either "a *proposed* record predating a section
may gain it — nothing is frozen until `accepted`", or "a record is judged on the
decision it made, not the template in force when it is accepted", whichever the
author means. Not a blocker: the issue as filed asked only about the eight accepted.

**NIT** `record-template.md:11-17` vs `:31-34` — the baseline will be written twice
and the template does not say so. `architecture-design` step 3 makes the
no-new-structure baseline one of the two-or-three compared approaches, and step 6
says to document the alternatives "each with the reason it was not chosen" — so when
the baseline loses it is both the `## Direct baseline` section and a `## Considered`
bullet. The level-3 template has the same overlap but partly disambiguates it in
`## Diagram` ("When the baseline won, the second graph is the strongest alternative
it beat"); level 2 has no such line. `122-a-record-persists-no-routing-state` shows
the shape working — its baseline won, and `Considered` lists only the three losing
options — but that is the easy direction.
→ Fix: one clause in `## Considered`, e.g. "the baseline has its own section; list it
here only if the reason it lost is not already there."

**NIT** `architecture-decisions/SKILL.md:131-133` — "`Direct baseline` arrived after

and carries the whole point.
→ Fix: generalise or drop the middle sentence — "no record written before it gained
one" — and keep the rule.
