Detail on why each DoD item is satisfied, since there's no spec dir to check
against:

- **Considered admits fit, not just fault** — both templates changed `<why it
  was not taken>` / `<why it fell>` to `<the reason it was not chosen>`, and
  both added the "fit and fault are both reasons" paragraph with a
  level-appropriate example (`driver 1` for architecture, `call site` for
  design). `architecture-design/SKILL.md` step 6 already said this (from
  #152); the templates previously disagreed with it and now don't — verified
  by grep that `why it fell`/`why it was not taken` are gone repo-wide, not
  just in the diffed hunks.
- **architecture-decisions gets a Direct baseline field** — added at
  `wfctl/agents/skills/architecture-decisions/record-template.md:11`, in the
  same position (immediately before `Decision`) as the level-3 template's
  existing one, with matching "hand-waving" warning language. `SKILL.md`'s
  "Writing one" section and verification checklist were both updated to name
  it, so the field list is consistent in three places (template, prose,
  checklist) rather than two.
- **The eight accepted records are handled, not silently skipped** — verified
  directly: `grep -l "Direct baseline" docs/architecture/*.md` returns nothing,
  and `grep -c "^status: accepted"` returns exactly 8, matching the issue's
  count. The diff doesn't backfill them; instead it adds a general rule to the
  immutability section of `architecture-decisions/SKILL.md` ("a record written
  before a section existed does not carry it, and is not backfilled") and
  explains why in terms of the same principle that already governs accepted
  records (no post-hoc edits). This reads as a real decision, written down in
  the one place a future reader asking "why doesn't this old record have X"
  would look — not silence, and not a scattered note in each of the 8 files.
  It's also durable: it answers the same question for the *next* field someone
  adds, not just this one.

  test_skill_cross_references.py::test_the_record_template_carries_every_section_a_record_needs`
