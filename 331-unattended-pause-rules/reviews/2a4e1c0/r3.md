BLOCKER  wfctl/agents/commands/speckit.clarify.md:L36-40 — the new trigger paragraph
extends *"Ask each question as step 4 renders it, **recommendation and all**"* to

question was never put at all.

the withdrawal rule below covers — put with their options and without a `**Recommended:**`
