Clarify, plan review, and analyze now save their scan files in the feature's spec folder, as `scans/<step>.md`, instead of committing them under `docs/architecture/scans/`. Your PRs no longer carry scan commits, and `wfctl store specs` saves the scans with the rest of the spec. `wfctl status` keeps a review step current until its scan file is there with a verdict and a coverage table, and a feature already past that step gets a warning instead. Signing off a plan edit writes to the spec folder and no longer asks you to commit, and `wfctl check-body` now fails when the PR body leaves out a plan sign-off's reason. Evaluation scans from a spike are unchanged.
