wfctl no longer ships the `using-superpowers`, `verification-before-completion`, or `brainstorm` skills, or their commands. The brainstorm step is listed once, as `/speckit.brainstorm`, so `speckit-brainstorm` no longer installs under `.claude/skills/`. `design-levels` and `python-pattern-selection` now point at `wfctl verify` where they named `verification-before-completion`. A project that already has the dropped skills keeps them until `wfctl install-skills --prune` runs, which `wfctl doctor` suggests and `/start-session` runs for you. The bob and copilot layers' copies go the same way, with `--agent bob` or `--agent copilot` added. A skill you wrote yourself is left alone.
