An architecture record can declare more than one diagram kind, as a list such as `diagram: [data-flow, sequence]`, and draw each one under its own `### <kind>` heading in `## Boundary`. `wfctl arch accept` refuses a declared kind with no drawing, a kind listed twice, and a heading for a kind the record doesn't declare, and `wfctl doctor` warns about each while the record is proposed. A record that declares one kind is checked exactly as before. The record template now opens with the decision and the drawing.
