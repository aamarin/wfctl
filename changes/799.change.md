A change that adds, removes, loosens, or tightens a security control, such as the worktree guard or a terminal check, now writes a threat model before any code. The threat model is a section of the change's architecture record, saying who the control defends against, which forms it must keep catching, and what it accepts. A guide walks the author through Shostack's four questions, and loads a domain guide only when the control reads an agent's commands or sits on a service or API. Security reviewers find that section and judge the change against it, instead of against a bar written into the review brief.
